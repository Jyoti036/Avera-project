Problem Statement

  • Many individuals lack awareness of adoption procedures, legal guidelines, and eligibility requirements.
  • Fragmented, unofficial information leads to social hesitation, fear, and misconceptions.
  • People who cannot adopt personally lack transparent and direct channels to support children living in care facilities.
  • Core Problem: A lack of accessible information and verifiable support avenues prevents community participation in child welfare.

Project Objectives

  • Increase community awareness regarding child adoption processes and rights.
  • Provide structured, official knowledge to guide prospective adoptive parents responsibly.
  • Enable supporters to fulfill direct care facility wishlists through item gifting.
  • Create a transparent feedback mechanism with proof of received support while safeguarding child privacy.

Unique Features of AVERA

1. Wishlist System

Care organizations post specific needed items (books, clothing, supplies). Donors fulfill actual needs directly, ensuring purposeful and non-redundant giving.

2. Milestone & Growth Updates

Periodic growth updates regarding children's development, health, and education milestones while maintaining ethical child data privacy.

3. Transparent Proof of Impact

Closing the loop: Care organizations confirm delivery with feedback messages and proof verification, reinforcing donor trust.
